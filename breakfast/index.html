How to Build a Healthy Breakfast in Iowa City Before Class

Four habits for mornings when you're running on five hours of sleep.

  1. Protein first. Eggs, Greek yogurt, or a protein smoothie keep you full longer than any pastry.
  2. Skip the syrup flood. Flavored lattes and syrupy toppings can add 300 calories before your first class.
  3. Pair carbs with protein. Oats, toast, or fruit plus eggs or yogurt beats carbs alone for a three-hour lecture.
  4. Order ahead. Most of these spots take online orders, so breakfast is waiting when you walk in.
