How to Find Cheap Healthy Food in Iowa City Without Overspending

Four habits that work at almost any counter downtown or in Coralville.

  1. Order the bowl, not the plate. Bowls usually cost $2 to $3 less and pile on more vegetables.
  2. Let beans do the heavy lifting. Beans and lentils are the cheapest protein on almost every menu.
  3. Skip the fountain drink. Water is free. A soda can add $2.50 and 250 calories to a $7 meal.
  4. Sauce on the side. You'll use about half as much, and the price stays exactly the same.
