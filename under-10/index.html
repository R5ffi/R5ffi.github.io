How to Find Cheap Healthy Food in Iowa City Without Overspending

These habits work at almost any counter downtown or in Coralville, not just the five spots above.

  • Order the bowl, not the plate. Bowls usually cost $2 to $3 less and pile on more vegetables.
  • Let beans and lentils do the work. They're the cheapest protein on almost every menu, and they keep you full for hours.
  • Skip the fountain drink. Water is free, and a soda can add $2.50 and 250 calories to a $7 meal.
  • Ask for sauce on the side. You'll use about half as much, which cuts calories without changing the price.
