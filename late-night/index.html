How to Order the Best Healthy Late Night Food in Iowa City

Small choices at the counter, big difference at 1 a.m.

  1. Go broth or bowl. Pho and poke usually have about half the calories of a fried late-night combo.
  2. Water first. Thirst can feel a lot like hunger after a long night.
  3. Swap the side. Fruit, slaw, or greens instead of fries. Most of our five spots do it for free.
  4. Save half for tomorrow. Late-night portions run big, and these orders reheat well.
