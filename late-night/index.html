How to Order the Best Healthy Late Night Food in Iowa City

A few small choices at the counter make a big difference after 10 p.m.

  • Go for broth or a bowl. Pho and poke bowls usually have about half the calories of a fried late-night combo.
  • Drink a glass of water first. Thirst can feel a lot like hunger after a long night.
  • Swap the side. Ask for fruit, slaw, or greens instead of fries or chips; most of our five spots do it for free.
  • Save half for tomorrow. Late-night portions run big, and most of these orders reheat well.
