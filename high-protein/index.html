Tips for Ordering High Protein Meals in Iowa City

Use these at any counter to push an ordinary order closer to your protein goal.

  • Double the protein, not the sauce. Extra chicken or tofu usually costs $2 to $3 and adds 20 grams or more.
  • Keep the carbs after a workout. Rice, quinoa, and farro help you refuel, so don't skip them out of habit.
  • Watch the creamy sauces. A ladle of ranch or aioli can add 200 calories and almost no protein, so ask for it on the side.
  • Plants count too. Tempeh, seitan, edamame, and lentils can stack up past 60 grams in a single meal.
