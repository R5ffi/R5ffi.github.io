Tips for Ordering High Protein Meals in Iowa City

Push any order closer to your protein goal.

  1. Double the protein, not the sauce. Extra chicken or tofu usually costs $2 to $3 and adds 20 grams or more.
  2. Keep the carbs after a workout. Rice, quinoa, and farro help you refuel, so don't skip them out of habit.
  3. Get creamy sauces on the side. A ladle of ranch or aioli adds about 200 calories and almost zero protein.
  4. Plants count too. Tempeh, seitan, edamame, and lentils can stack past 60 grams in one meal.
